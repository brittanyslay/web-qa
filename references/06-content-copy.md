# Gate 6 - Content, Copy & Legal

Read it out loud. Then read it again tomorrow. Typos on a homepage cost more trust than a slow load.

## Factual accuracy (verify against a source - never assume)
- [ ] **Business name spelled correctly everywhere**, including title tags and alt text
- [ ] Phone number - call it
- [ ] Email address - send to it
- [ ] Physical address + map pin correct; suite number included
- [ ] Hours of operation, including holidays and time zone
- [ ] **Prices, packages, and what's included** - cross-check against the client's current sheet
- [ ] Dates, event times, deadlines, and time zones (check AM/PM and the year)
- [ ] Staff names, titles, pronouns, credentials, and headshots matched to the right person
- [ ] Client/partner names and logos spelled and rendered correctly, with permission to use
- [ ] Statistics and claims are sourced and current; no stale "since 2019" copy
- [ ] Social handles link to the right accounts and those accounts are active
- [ ] Nothing from the template or a previous client left in ("Lorem ipsum," "Your Company Here," placeholder bios/photos)

## Writing quality
- [ ] Spellcheck **and** a human read - spellcheck misses "manager" vs "manger," "pubic" vs "public"
- [ ] Consistent voice and tense across pages
- [ ] Consistent conventions: serial comma, capitalization style, date format, number style, em dashes, curly quotes
- [ ] Headings actually describe the section below them
- [ ] No jargon the audience won't know; target ~10th-grade reading level unless the audience demands otherwise
- [ ] Scannable - short paragraphs, meaningful subheads, no wall of text
- [ ] CTA copy is specific and consistent ("Get tickets," not "Submit")
- [ ] Microcopy present where users hesitate (form help text, "no spam," "takes 2 minutes")
- [ ] Error/empty/success messages are written, not defaults
- [ ] Alt text is descriptive, not "image1.jpg"
- [ ] Inclusive language; correct pronouns; no assumed gender

## Design/content fit
- [ ] Real content used, not lorem - layouts tested with the longest and shortest realistic values
- [ ] Image quality consistent; no pixelated, stretched, or mismatched-style photos
- [ ] Photos are licensed or owned - no unlicensed stock, no Google Images grabs
- [ ] Brand palette, fonts, and logo usage match the style guide exactly
- [ ] Logo has clear space and correct variant on each background
- [ ] Text over images stays readable at every viewport (worst-case crop)
- [ ] Icons consistent in style and weight

## Legal & policy
- [ ] Privacy policy present, current, and linked in the footer + at data collection points
- [ ] Terms of service / terms of use where applicable
- [ ] Cookie notice + consent if serving EU/UK/CA users, with a real opt-out that works
- [ ] Copyright line with the **current year** (auto-generate it)
- [ ] Accessibility statement if required by the client's sector
- [ ] Required disclaimers (medical, legal, financial, results-not-typical, affiliate disclosure)
- [ ] Refund/shipping/cancellation policy for anything transactional
- [ ] Third-party content credited and licensed (fonts, icons, photos, code)
- [ ] Trademark/registered marks used correctly
- [ ] Nothing defamatory, and no client/customer info published without consent
- [ ] No personal home address, personal phone, or private data exposed unintentionally
